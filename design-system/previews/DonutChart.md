Donut chart: a part-to-whole ring with the headline figure in the centre — use for budget utilisation and cost mix.

**Consumer provides:** segments keyed by measure or cost category, the centre value and its caption.

- Utilisation: Spent `m-actual`, Committed `m-committed`, Available `chart-other`.
- Cost mix: `ct-labour`, `ct-materials`, `ct-plant`, `ct-overheads`, `ct-other`, in that order.
- Centre value in `kpi`, caption in `ink-muted`; ring 32px wide; 2px `surface` gaps.
