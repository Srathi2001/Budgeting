Pie chart for a part-to-whole split of one total, at most five slices — the standard view is spend by division.

**Consumer provides:** slices keyed by division (or by cost category), with values.

- Division slices take their fixed `dv-<key>` colour and stay in registry order from 12 o'clock (Contracting first), so the same division sits in the same place on every pie.
- Cost-category pies use the fixed `ct-*` colours in registry order (Labour, Materials, Plant & fleet, Overheads, Other).
- 2px `surface` gaps, percentage labels on leader lines, legend repeats name and percentage.
